**Capsid 1.1.6: safer unpairing, no lost Working Notes at quit, and fixes from a code review.**

**Unpairing a phone takes effect at once.** When you remove a device in **Settings → Phone**, or unpair from the phone itself, it can't reach your Mac from that moment, even if Capsid couldn't save its list of devices. It stays removed after Capsid restarts. If the list couldn't be saved, Settings → Phone says so until Capsid saves it. A photo or message still uploading from a device you remove is stopped before it reaches the session.

**Quitting won't lose a Working Note.** If a note you just typed couldn't be saved, Capsid now asks before quitting, the same way it already did for Shelf legends. Choose Cancel to keep Capsid open, with your text still in the panel and a Retry button.

**Ask Another Model.** Asking a new question while the last answer was still being sent to the session no longer marks the new answer as Sent.

**Shelf.** Closing the Shelf stops its search of your sessions' files, and opening it again searches fresh, so old results don't come back. Thumbnails still being drawn when you close the Shelf are dropped instead of filling it up again. Page thumbnails are cropped in the background.

**Compare.** When one version adds or removes a single block of more than 2,000 lines (an empty file against a long one, say), Compare now says there are too many changes, as it does for any other comparison past that limit, instead of listing them all.

**Browser window.** The button that sends a page to your browser now says **Open in Browser**, because it opens your default browser, which may not be Safari.

Capsid 1.1.6 needs macOS 27.

**First launch.** Capsid isn't notarized by Apple, so if you download Bench.zip
through a browser, macOS blocks it the first time. Open it once, click
**Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to
**Security**, click **Open Anyway** next to Capsid and confirm. Right-click →
Open no longer gets past this on current macOS. Installing or updating with
the [one-line install](https://github.com/samporter14/bench#easiest-one-line-in-terminal),
or with Capsid's own **Install and Reopen**, skips this step.
